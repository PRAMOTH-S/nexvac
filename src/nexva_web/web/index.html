Motor limits (ESP32 PID)

The PWM band the wheel controller is allowed to use, and the speed cap. Min PWM is where the wheels actually break away - below it the motors buzz without turning. Raise it on thick carpet or a low battery; lower it if the robot creeps when told to stop. Applied live, no reflash.
using firmware defaults
The ESP32 reverts to its compiled defaults on every reboot, so these are resent automatically every few seconds once you have applied them.

Power & throttling

The signal you get nowhere else. A Pi that browns out under motor load caps its own clock and keeps running, so it presents as "the software got slow" rather than as a power fault. Since boot is the half that matters: a dip during a hard turn lasts milliseconds and no poll will ever catch it live.

IMU & motion check

Live from /imu, about twice a second. With no fresh sample the numbers are blanked, never left on screen. In accel-only mode the sensor has no heading, and the card says so instead of showing one.
